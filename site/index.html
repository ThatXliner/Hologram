New in 1.2.0

Taste that carries over.
Evidence you can rerun.

AutoCull now keeps learning from one profile across every folder you open, and gives you the data to check how it learns.

01AutoCull

Your taste profile follows you between shoots.

Your saved picks, rejects, ratings, and comparisons carry across folders with your active profile. Keep teaching AutoCull as you review each shoot.

  • Your manual reviews teach it. Its own automatic picks don't.
  • Corrections and undo change what it learns.
02Experimental · Opt-in

A native XGBoost learner, if you want to try it.

Try Use XGBoost learner under Analysis & taste profile. It trains locally with no extra setup. The built-in learner stays the default.

ExperimentalWe haven't established that it ranks better on held-out human reviews.

03Export

Decision books for repeatable evaluation.

Export a decision book and Hologram generates a JSON file from your saved reviews and derived image features. Compare trainers against the same decisions without relabeling your photos.

  • Anonymous photo IDs, with no original images or file paths.
  • Automatic decisions stay out of the review data.
